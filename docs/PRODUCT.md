# PRODUCT.md — current product reference

> What the product does today, backed by the implemented code. Durable customer-facing policy only — not roadmap (`PRODUCT_PLAN.md`) or engineering plan (`PLAN.md`). Technical rationale lives in `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `FLOW.md`, and `DECISIONS.md`.

## Customers and core loop

Malaysian event hosts (weddings first, then other occasions) buy a hosted digital invitation card:

1. Browse Template showcase cards at `/templates`.
2. Customise the card through Section-stack editing with an inline Live preview.
3. Pay the stored MYR price through Stripe Checkout, Bank DuitNow or Touch ’n Go. Manual QR payments require receipt evidence and administrator review; partial payments need a same-method top-up.
4. Review the paid draft, explicitly acknowledge publication, and publish it.
5. If a Published invitation needs a correction, an administrator may open one
   fixed seven-day edit window; the owner edits the saved revision and explicitly
   republishes it.
6. If a paid predecessor needs a controlled replacement, an administrator may
   grant a separately entitled replacement draft and explicitly complete its
   audited cutover.
7. Share the published card URL `/i/<slug>` with guests.
8. Guests RSVP; the owner reads responses in the dashboard.

## Supported capabilities

- **Template preset selection.** The catalogue offers curated per-occasion presets (Section-v2). A preset supplies the default Gaya swatch (the shared theme values) and an ordered Section stack. Every active template is Section-v2 since migration 009.
- **Section-stack customisation.** In the three-pane editor the customer toggles Sections on/off, reorders them, adds catalogue Sections, edits each Section's fields in schema-driven sub-forms (with per-Section custom forms where needed), picks a Variant per Section from the Variant gallery (recommended-first for the occasion), and uploads Section images. Customer images remain in private Storage, are served through an app-controlled card-scoped endpoint, and are visible to the owner/admin while the card is editable; uploads are bounded by supported image types and size. Drafts persist locally plus to `cards.custom_data_draft` via a debounced save, so work survives reloads and image upload works before payment and during manual payment review.
- **Gaya panel.** A card-level style picker of curated Gaya swatches (palette + fonts + background + animation) appropriate to the template's type; any Gaya swatch is selectable on any template, recommendations only order/badge. Selecting a Gaya swatch re-themes the whole card cohesively. The panel has no custom controls — customers can only choose from these curated Gaya swatches; raw palette/font editing and a "Custom" Gaya are not supported. The approved palette control is that set of 14 curated presets in `lib/themes/presets.ts`, and the approved font controls are only the fonts those presets apply (Playfair Display, Cormorant, Great Vibes, Cinzel, Fraunces, Dancing Script, and Bodoni Moda for display/script slots, Plus Jakarta Sans for body) — see D15.
- **Section editor tour (desktop).** First-time customers get a five-step Malay guided tour of the editor. The tour opens once per account and remains available from the Bantuan control.
- **Payment and publication.** A pending order and unpublished draft exist before checkout. Stripe and manual QR methods are mutually exclusive while a receipt awaits review or a manual amount is active. A Stripe-to-QR handoff requires Stripe-confirmed expiry of the exact saved Session and release of its reservation. The first sufficient confirmed payment by Stripe, Bank DuitNow, or Touch ’n Go marks the order paid and opens exactly one editable private paid draft; it never publishes, exposes a guest URL, or sends a publication email. The owner can edit invitation text, layout, the Gaya panel, Sections and images before and during manual receipt review (submitted, insufficient and same-method top-up), and after payment until first publication, while the selected template and order price stay fixed. Publication is a separate owner action using the exact acknowledgement `I understand that this invitation will become public and read-only.` and publishes the latest successfully saved draft. The first publication sets `published_at` and starts hosting; no automatic paid-draft expiry or hosting duration is inferred. Partial payments do not publish; Stripe cannot collect their balance. The Checkout status panel shows the winning method/time and current safe payment state.
- **Administrator-approved revision windows.** After first publication, only an administrator can open a fixed seven-day edit window with a bounded reason. The grant starts at the database approval time, is audited with actor/time/reason, atomically makes the same slug private, and preserves the first snapshot, payment evidence, template entitlement and original `published_at`. The owner may edit existing content, reorder/change Section variants, use the Gaya panel and append new image UUIDs during the active window; template replacement, retirement, refunds and new purchase are not part of this flow. The owner must acknowledge and republish the latest saved valid revision with the expected version. Republish closes the window, creates an immutable later publication and restores the same slug as read-only. An expired window keeps the invitation private and locked; the draft is retained and only a separately audited administrator grant can reopen it. Duplicate grant retries do not restart the deadline, and conflicting grants serialize/reject.
- **Mohon Suntingan requests.** The owner can submit one bounded reason for an own Published or Edit window expired invitation from the Dashboard. Submission is an in-app request only: a Published invitation stays public and read-only, while an expired invitation stays private and locked; the owner sees a pending, rejected or stale customer-safe status. The focused Admin queue shows pending counts and lets an administrator approve or reject; rejection requires a customer-readable reason. Approval rechecks payment, ownership, availability, lifecycle, publication and generation eligibility in one database transaction before delegating the existing seven-day edit-window grant. Expired invitations open a new seven-day window only after approval. Exact retries are idempotent, never create duplicate windows or extend deadlines, and changed/disabled/retired/replaced requests become stale rather than granting a different revision. This flow sends no email, WhatsApp message or external notification.
- **Controlled replacement and retirement.** An administrator may grant one reasoned, audited, replay-safe replacement for a paid predecessor with no direct successor. The replacement has its own order, slug, invitation and grant-only entitlement, keeps the same owner/template, and copies the latest published snapshot with target-card-scoped image references. The target remains owner-editable while pending and may upload additional target-scoped images, but it cannot take payment, upload a receipt, show payment-confirmation metadata, use the ordinary paid-draft publication route or receive a new edit-window grant. A still-public predecessor may be disabled for moderation while preparation is pending, but neither a pending target nor a retired predecessor can be restored or published through ordinary controls. The administrator must complete a version- and asset-validated atomic cutover; the predecessor then becomes terminal **Retired**. Orders, payment evidence, publication history, source assets and RSVPs stay with their original cards; no RSVP is copied or moved. Owner/admin surfaces show the reason and lineage. A Retired guest URL is generic unavailable only across HTML/RSC, metadata, OG, RSVP, realtime and asset access. Completed replacements may be predecessors for later replacement chains.
- **Manual QR review.** Administrators manage one Bank QR and one Touch ’n Go QR, with image uploads at `/admin/settings/qr`. Customer payment starts with a Stripe/DuitNow QR modal; the dedicated QR page provides Bank and Touch ’n Go choices. `/admin` shows a pending-review notification and `/admin/payments` provides a newest-first table with pending, insufficient, rejected, confirmed and duplicate filters. Receipt versions, payment-time invitation snapshots, evidence and review/correction rows are immutable. Rejection and insufficient decisions require reasons; insufficient review also requires private administrator evidence. Replacement keeps the same attempt and prior files. Each partial top-up has a new attempt and required receipt. The live invitation draft and its images remain editable during review, while payment audit protects the order and fulfilment record from deletion. Administrators can inspect all receipt versions, private decision evidence and record reasoned quarantine/removal tombstones, including for previously reviewed files.
- **Invitation lifecycle.** Every invitation is in exactly one of eight derived states, shown on the dashboard: Editing (still being customised — edit remains available; delete is available only when no Checkout/payment-audit guard is active), Paid draft (the first confirmed payment is complete — the owner may edit the unpublished draft, but no guest URL exists), Publishing (a legacy/recovery compatibility state for a paid row not yet normalized to the paid-draft entitlement), Published (live and read-only for guests), Edit window (an administrator-approved seven-day revision is private and owner-editable), Edit window expired (private, draft retained, locked until a new grant), Unavailable (administratively disabled; the recorded reason is shown with a support contact), or Retired (a controlled-replacement predecessor; terminal, reasoned and lineage-visible to the owner/admin, with generic guest unavailability). A pending controlled-replacement target is still an owner-editable paid draft, but its grant-only entitlement blocks ordinary publication, payment/receipt flows, new edit-window grants and destructive lineage writes. The payment guard is not a lifecycle state: an open Session is confirmed expired before deletion, and an ambiguous Session remains protected. A paid draft never expires automatically. Publication is owner-confirmed and separate from payment, except that controlled-replacement publication can occur only through administrator cutover; the first snapshot and every later publication are immutable. Admin disable always prevails over a window or publication, and restore never republishes old content or bypasses the window rules. The admin dashboard exposes payment-confirmed time, days since payment, lifecycle state, and last successful customer draft-save activity; grant-only replacements intentionally show no payment-confirmed time or revenue/payment evidence; old rows may have no activity timestamp.
- **Dashboard organisation.** Small phone renders form a responsive invitation gallery. Perlu perhatian holds private and unavailable states; Akan datang holds published current/future and undated invitations; Majlis lepas holds fully past published invitations. Sticky selected filters show one group at a time, with search by name, invitation link or displayed date and six invitations per page. One primary action follows the invitation lifecycle. Published tiles show attending guests and response totals, with the full breakdown in RSVP. Payment details remain accessible on demand.
- **Guest experience.** Only the selected current Published publication is reachable by guests. Publishing, Edit window, Edit window expired, Unavailable and Retired slugs render no prior invitation data — page HTML/RSC, metadata, link previews, RSVP and guestbook realtime all use one boundary. During a window the same slug shows only `{Customer name} is making edits. Please wait.` with a safe bounded profile name; after expiry it remains private. Guests can load only exact customer images referenced by the current immutable publication; superseded, orphan and draft-only assets remain private. RSVP is open to guests without login only for that current publication, and wishes/messages are scoped to its publication realtime topic. Bytes already downloaded or stale third-party caches cannot be revoked by this application.
- **Informational premium markers.** Some Variants are marked premium in the Variant gallery; today this is informational labelling, not paid gating.

## Explicitly deferred / not supported

- Automated payment verification, automated refunds, chargebacks and settlement reconciliation. Receipt evidence is reviewed manually (D18, amended by #69).
- More than two DuitNow slots; dynamic slot creation/deletion (D18).
- Customer restore, archive, template replacement, refunds, or new purchase as part of a revision window. Availability changes remain reasoned administrator actions, and the edit-window exception is limited to issue #74; controlled replacement and retirement are administrator-only as described above.
- Guest management, delivery tracking of invitations, response rates, and no-response counts.
- Paid premium-variant gating beyond informational markers (see `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` Q3).
- Multi-language structured text fields — invitation content is plain strings with manual bilingual copy per the approved language model (D14).
- Free-form visual/drag canvas editing; rich-text/markdown content; multi-language rendering engines.
- Schema-in-database Section authoring — Section schemas are code-first (D2).
- Custom card theme preset — raw palette/font controls or a "Custom" Gaya in the Gaya panel (see D15; open follow-up in `TEMPLATE_GAYA.md` §9).
- Non-Malay tour text — the tour is plain-Malay-only by decision (D16); field-level guidance inside the five surfaces is also out of scope. (Mobile tour support moved into scope with KJ-2; the tour now has a mobile step list over the canvas-first shell.)

## Receipt payment policy (issues #69 and #70)

Bank DuitNow and separate Touch ’n Go payments require private receipt evidence; transfer references are optional. Customers may replace incorrect receipts before confirmation. Administrators manually record received amounts and rejection reasons in an append-only ledger. Insufficient payment records credited amount, server-calculated remaining amount and private administrator evidence. Partial balances use the same QR method and a new receipt; Stripe fallback is available only after rejection with no confirmed manual amount. The first sufficient total opens one paid draft; overpayments and duplicates go to manual support and never create another entitlement or email. A duplicate correction requires a reason and appends a new row. Current and prior files are retained indefinitely; only authorized administrators may quarantine/remove them for harmful-file, legal or operational reasons, preserving permanent metadata. This supersedes earlier reference-only limitations.
